## 🚀 Selected Projects
### Professional & client work
**[NIMECHE OAU](https://nimeche-oau.vercel.app)** — Official website for the Department of Mechanical Engineering, Obafemi Awolowo University.
**[Fischerbon](https://fischerbon.vercel.app)** — Learning management portal for Fischerbon Engineering Ltd, built to modernise their CAD training workflow with structured course delivery and student tooling.
**[Zorvyn](https://fin-dashboard-ui.vercel.app)** — Fintech dashboard prototype built during a frontend internship: data visualisation, responsive layouts, and enterprise-level UI detail.
**[iP Hive](https://iphive.vercel.app)** — Innovation protection platform built with cross-functional teams, giving inventors verified idea stewardship and trust-first onboarding.
**[AmtaPro](https://amtapro.vercel.app)** — Networking platform for football talent: highlight showcases, professional player profiles, and direct connection to scouts and clubs.
### Hackathon builds
**[Baseline](https://abaseline.vercel.app)** — Live drift-alert dashboard for community health workers, built on the Ontomorph digital twin and the HOLON clinical knowledge API. *Ontomorph Hackathon.*
**[ClinkerFlow](https://clinkerflow-topaz.vercel.app)** — Operational intelligence framework that maps, measures and optimises every stage of clinker-to-cement conversion to cut production losses. *DCP University Hackathon.*
**[Suhoor](https://suhoor-group.web.app)** — Coordination platform for Ramadan communities: instant scheduling and group sync for Suhoor groups. *Hackathon winner.*
### Products & experiments
**[Seej](https://seejshop.web.app)** — SEO-optimised e-commerce storefront in Next.js, built for fast product discovery and conversion.
**[MEE 206](https://mee206.web.app)** — Study companion for Engineering Dynamics: structured lessons, resources and practice flows.
**[Khushu](https://khushu-one.vercel.app)** — Salah training experience combining audio, video and interactive guidance.
**[Sniply](https://snip-ly-sigma.vercel.app)** — Fast URL shortener with a minimal, distraction-free interface.
**[FastTypist](https://fast-typist.mygamesonline.org/)** — Typing trainer built for speed and accuracy, with instant feedback.
**[Heroship](https://heroship.appwrite.network)** — Open-source hero section generator for rapid UI production.
